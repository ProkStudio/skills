# What a settlement contains

## Program by scale

| Scale | Buildings | Must-have non-residential |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hamlet | 5-8 | Well, barn or mill, shrine or communal fire |
| Village | 10-25 | Well/fountain, church or shrine, smithy, inn, farm buildings, market spot |
| Town | 25-60 | Square with market hall, church, inn(s), smithy, bakery, guardhouse, mill, school/library, docks or warehouse, graveyard, stables |
| City | 60+ | Districts, cathedral/great temple, keep or town hall, guild halls, barracks, hospital, harbour, industrial quarter, parks, slums, walls and gates, bridges |

A settlement with houses only is the most common failure. Aim for roughly 1 non-residential
building per 4-6 houses in a village, 1 per 3 in a town core.

## Land-use ratios (by built area)

| Use | Share | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residential | 50-60% | Densest at the centre, loosest at the edge |
| Commercial / market | 10-15% | On the main road and around the square; shops with living space above |
| Civic & religious | 10% | Gets the best sites, the tallest form and the best material |
| Industrial / agricultural | 15% | Downhill, downstream, downwind, or by the water/rail |
| Green & public | 10% | Square, churchyard, gardens, parks, orchards |

## District types

| District | Density | Heights | Palette shift | Signature elements |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old core | Very high, party walls | 2-4 floors | Oldest materials, most weathering | Crooked lanes, jettied upper floors, arches over alleys |
| Market quarter | High | 2-3 | Brighter accents, banners | Market hall, stalls, awnings, crates, weighhouse |
| Civic / temple | Low coverage, big mass | Tall single volumes | Best stone, gold/copper accents | Steps, plazas, columns, statues, bell tower |
| Residential mid | Medium | 1-3 | Main palette | Gardens, fences, laundry, wells, small shops on corners |
| Wealthy quarter | Low, set back | 2-3 + towers | Cleaner, lighter, more glass | Walled gardens, gates, coach houses, ornament |
| Slums / outskirts | Chaotic, high | 1-2 + lean-tos | Mismatched, patched | Shacks, scaffolding, tarps (wool/banners), mud, rubbish |
| Industrial | Big footprints | Tall chimneys | Dark, sooty, iron/copper | Mill, kiln, tannery, smelter, chimneys, spoil heaps, cranes |
| Docks / harbour | Medium | 2-3 warehouses | Wood + tar + stone quay | Piers, cranes, boats, nets, barrels, fish market |
| Agricultural fringe | Very low | 1-2 | Wood, thatch, mud | Farmhouses, barns, silos, fields, orchards, pastures |
| Military | Low | Walls + keep | Heaviest stone | Barracks, armoury, training yard, stables, wall towers |

## Placement logic

- **Civic and religious** on the highest or most visible ground, facing the square, closing a
  street view.
- **Commerce** follows traffic: main road, square edges, gates, bridge heads, quay.
- **Industry** near its resource and away from the wealthy: mill on the river, mine buildings
  at the shaft, tannery downstream, smelter downwind, kiln at the clay pit.
- **Farms** on the best soil outside the built area; pastures on the poorer slopes.
- **Storage** between production and transport (warehouse between mill and quay).
- **Graveyard** beside the church or just outside the wall, walled, with a gate and a chapel.
- **Slums** in the leftovers: steep ground, the flood plain, against the wall, behind the
  industrial quarter.

## Building types and rough footprints

| Type | Footprint | Floors | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cottage | 5x7 - 7x9 | 1-2 | Garden, woodpile, chimney |
| Town house | 5x9 - 7x11 | 2-3 | Party walls, shop or storage on the ground floor |
| Shop + dwelling | 7x9 | 2-3 | Wide ground-floor opening, hanging sign, awning |
| Inn / tavern | 11x15 | 2-3 | Stable yard, arch to the yard, sign, many chimneys |
| Smithy | 7x11 | 1-2 + loft | Open forge side, chimney, coal pile, water trough |
| Bakery | 7x9 | 2 | Oven bulge, chimney, flour sacks |
| Mill (water) | 9x11 | 2-3 | Wheel, race, sluice, sack hoist dormer |
| Mill (wind) | 9x9 round | 3-5 | Cap and sails, mound, cart track |
| Church / temple | 11x21 - 15x35 | Tall nave + tower | Axis, churchyard, porch, bells |
| Town hall | 13x17 | 2-3 | Arcade on the ground floor, clock, steps, flags |
| Warehouse | 11x21 | 2-3 | Loading doors, hoist beam, minimal windows |
| Barracks | 9x25 | 2 | Repetitive windows, yard, armoury |
| Keep | 15x15 - 21x21 | 4-6 | Batter at the base, few low openings, hoardings |
| Barn | 9x15 | 1 + loft | Big doors, ramp, hay, fenced yard |

## Naming and signage

Give the streets and buildings names on signs - "Tanner's Lane", "The Broken Oar". A named
town reads as inhabited, and it forces you to justify each district. Use hanging signs over
shop doors, notice boards in the square, and item frames as trade symbols (bread, anvil,
potion) for shops.
