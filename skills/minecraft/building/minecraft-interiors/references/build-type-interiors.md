# Interiors by build type

Each entry: program, focal point, palette direction, signature props, lighting.

## Cottage / survival starter

- Program: single room or two - hearth + bed + kitchen corner + storage wall; ladder to attic.
- Focal: the hearth, centred on the wall opposite the door.
- Palette: oak/spruce planks, stripped logs, cobble base, wool accent.
- Props: barrel counter, cauldron sink, crates, hay, herb pots in the window, drying rack
  (trapdoors + item frames).
- Lighting: campfire + 2 lanterns + hidden glowstone under the carpet.

## Manor / noble house

- Program: entry hall with stair, drawing room, dining room, study, kitchen wing, 3-4 bedrooms,
  servant stair, cellar.
- Focal: the stair in the hall; then the dining chandelier.
- Palette: dark oak + smooth quartz/white concrete + polished diorite; gold or red accents.
- Props: long table with candles, portrait wall (paintings), bookshelf wall with lecterns,
  banners in the accent colour, carpet runners on the stair.
- Lighting: chandeliers, wall sconces every 5, cove light in coffers.

## Tavern / inn

- Program: hall with bar (60%), booths and long tables, hearth, kitchen behind the bar, stair
  to 3-5 guest rooms, cellar of barrels.
- Focal: the bar back wall - bottle rack, kegs, chalkboard sign.
- Palette: spruce + dark oak + bricks; brass/copper accents.
- Props: barrels laid sideways with trapdoor taps, potions in item frames, stew bowls on
  tables, hanging signs, dart board (target block), stage with note blocks.
- Lighting: warm and uneven - lanterns on chains over the bar, candles on tables, hearth glow.

## Shop

- Program: entry, display floor, counter separating customer and staff, back stock room.
- Focal: back wall display or the counter.
- Palette: match the exterior; one saturated accent for the brand.
- Props: goods on shelves and in item frames, chiseled bookshelves as pigeonholes, price signs,
  crates by the door, a plant, a till (lectern or decorated pot).
- Lighting: bright and even over the goods (hidden under-carpet and cove), warmer at the counter.

## Library / archive

- Program: nave of shelving, reading tables under high windows, mezzanine gallery, archive
  room, a spiral or straight stair.
- Focal: reading table under the biggest window, or a globe/model on a plinth.
- Palette: dark oak + chiseled bookshelf + stone bricks; green or oxblood textiles.
- Props: chiseled bookshelves with varied fill, lecterns, ladders on rails (ladder + chain),
  scroll baskets, candles with glass covers, a cat bed.
- Lighting: cool daylight from clerestory windows + warm task light at tables. Keep the
  shelving slightly dimmer than the tables.

## Castle great hall / throne room

- Program: axis from the entry to the throne, side aisles, high table, banner bays, musician
  gallery, service door to kitchens.
- Focal: the throne on a 3-5 step plinth, framed by banners and a window behind.
- Palette: stone bricks + deepslate + polished blackstone; gold blocks and coloured banners.
- Props: banners in every bay, braziers (campfire in a cauldron-like frame of iron bars),
  long tables, weapon racks, chandeliers of iron bars and lanterns, carpet runner up the axis.
- Lighting: braziers along the aisles, hidden light in the vault ribs, brightest on the throne.

## Temple / shrine

- Program: approach, threshold, nave or courtyard, altar, side chapels or offering niches,
  crypt below.
- Focal: altar at the end of the axis, lit from above.
- Palette: calcite/quartz + deepslate + copper (oxidised for age); one sacred accent colour.
- Props: candles in odd groups, banners, decorated pots as offerings, water basin, bells
  (from copper blocks or a real bell), amethyst for arcane variants.
- Lighting: one dramatic shaft of light on the altar, everything else dim; soul lanterns for
  a cold cult, ochre froglights for a warm one.

## Smithy / workshop

- Program: forge zone, anvil zone, quench barrel, tool wall, storage, display of finished
  goods, sleeping loft.
- Focal: the forge with visible fire.
- Palette: blackstone/basalt gradient + bricks + iron/copper blocks; soot darkening near the fire.
- Props: anvils, grindstone, smithing table, tools in item frames, armor stands with armor,
  barrels of coal (black concrete/coal blocks), chains, bellows (trapdoors + leather look).
- Lighting: fire-motivated - lava behind bars, campfires, magma blocks in the floor near the
  forge, dim elsewhere.

## Storage / tech base room

- Program: sorted barrel/chest wall, item frame labels, input hoppers, overflow, walking aisle
  2 wide, crafting station at the end.
- Focal: the labelled wall itself - make the grid perfect and the labels legible.
- Palette: smooth stone + iron blocks + deepslate tiles; one colour per category.
- Props: item frames (one per barrel), signs, hoppers, redstone hidden behind a false wall,
  copper grates as vents (1.21).
- Lighting: even and shadowless - under-carpet or cove; never block access with fixtures.

## Ship cabin / boat interior

- Program: 3-block clearance max, stern windows, captain's desk, bunk, map table, cargo hold
  below with barrels.
- Focal: stern window wall.
- Palette: dark oak + spruce + copper; ropes from chains.
- Props: map in item frames, lectern with a book, lantern swinging on a chain, hammock (wool
  slabs between fence posts), barrels lashed with chains.
- Lighting: one hanging lantern + hidden light under the bunk; keep it moody.

## Modern apartment / minimal house

- Program: open plan living/kitchen/dining, one clean circulation line, bedroom and bathroom
  behind a single wall, terrace.
- Focal: the window wall and the view; second, a low sofa group on a rug.
- Palette: smooth quartz/white concrete + polished deepslate/andesite + warm wood accent.
- Props: low furniture (slabs, carpets), a large flat art wall (paintings), potted big dripleaf,
  glass-panel kitchen counter, copper bulb pendants (1.21).
- Lighting: cove strips, end rods recessed in the ceiling, no visible flame.
