# Room programs, sizes and circulation

All numbers are interior dimensions in blocks (walls excluded) and clearance is floor surface
to ceiling underside. "Min" is the smallest size that still reads correctly; "comfortable" is
what to aim for when space allows.

## Adjacency rules

- Public -> semi-public -> private. Entry touches hall/living; kitchen touches dining;
  bedrooms sit at the end of the chain or on an upper floor.
- Wet rooms cluster: bathroom next to kitchen or above it, so plumbing props share a wall.
- Noisy/dirty functions get a buffer: smithy, stable and farm room never open straight into a
  bedroom.
- Storage sits where it is used: pantry off the kitchen, tool store off the workshop, chest
  hall next to the entry in a survival base.
- Vertical logic: heavy/dirty low (cellar, workshop, storage), living in the middle, sleeping
  and studies high.

## Circulation

- Corridors 2 wide for cottages, 3 for manors, 4+ only for civic buildings.
- Keep 50-60% of each floor walkable. A 7x9 living room should still let the player cross it
  diagonally without jumping.
- No room should be the only route to two other rooms unless it is a hall by design.
- Doors: 2-wide openings for the main entrance and between public rooms; 1-wide doors for
  private rooms. Doors open into the smaller/less used room.
- Stairs: 1-wide stairs only in cottages; 2-wide with a railing elsewhere. Landing 2x3, and
  3 blocks of headroom above every step.
- Dead ends need a payoff - window, art, alcove, plant - or they read as unfinished.

## Sight lines

Stand in every doorway and check what the player sees:

1. The focal point of that room, or
2. A framed view through a window, or
3. An enfilade - aligned doorways ending on something worth seeing.

If the answer is "a blank wall" or "a chest side", move the props or the door.

## Scale anchors

- Player is 1 wide, 2 tall (1.8), fits through a 1x2 gap.
- A bed occupies 1x2; leave 1 free block on the access side.
- A dining chair + table needs 3 blocks of depth per side.
- A counter run is 1 deep; an island needs 1 clear block all around.
- Grand rooms need 2-3 block trim and 2-block-wide pilasters; 1-block trim disappears above
  ~8 blocks of height.
