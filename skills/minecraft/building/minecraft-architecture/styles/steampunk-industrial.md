# Steampunk / industrial

Brick and blackstone mass, copper and iron machinery, pipes, stacks, glazed sawtooth roofs.
The mechanical layer is the ornament.

## Signature

- Heavy masonry base with large arched openings, light steel-and-glass structure above.
- Copper in all oxidation states as the colour story.
- Functional-looking clutter: pipes, tanks, gauges, chains, walkways, smokestacks.
- Structure and services exposed rather than hidden.

## Massing

- Big simple main volume (hall, workshop, factory floor) plus tall thin verticals - chimneys,
  water towers, clock towers, cranes.
- Repetition with rhythm: 5-9 identical bays of arched windows, broken once by a gate bay.
- Add-on logic: annexes, boiler house, loading bay, catwalks bridging between masses.
- Vertical break is mandatory: a smokestack at least 1.8x the roof height.

## Roofs

Primary: sawtooth (glazed on one side) or monitor for big spans; flat with a parapet for
urban blocks; gambrel for warehouses; domes and helm roofs for observatory or clock towers.

- Sawtooth: repeat a steep glazed face + a shallow roof plane every 5-7 blocks; glaze with
  panes framed in iron bars, all jambs full blocks.
- Monitor: raise a central band 2-3 blocks with clerestory glazing and louvres (trapdoors).
- Roof furniture: vents (copper grate + cauldron), tanks (copper blocks + chains), fans,
  antennae (lightning rods), catwalks of iron bars and slabs.
- Parapets in brick with a copper capping course.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Base | bricks, blackstone, polished blackstone, deepslate bricks, nether bricks for stacks, tuff bricks (1.21+) |
| Secondary | copper block and all oxidation stages, chiseled copper, copper grate, cut copper stairs/slabs (1.21+) |
| Metal | iron blocks, iron bars, iron trapdoors, chains, anvils, hoppers, lightning rods |
| Dark trim | polished deepslate, cobbled deepslate, basalt, polished basalt |
| Glazing | glass panes, tinted glass, gray stained glass |
| Light | copper bulbs (1.21+), lanterns, redstone lamps, soul lanterns, froglight for gauges |

Copper gradient: oxidized high up and where water runs, exposed and weathered in the middle,
fresh copper on recently "replaced" parts. Wax to freeze the look.

## Details

- Pipes: waxed copper block runs with cauldrons as joints, chains as thin conduits, hoppers
  as funnels and reducers; route them along walls and turn corners with stairs.
- Boilers and tanks: 3x3x4 copper or iron block cylinders with chain hoops and a gauge
  (item frame with a clock or compass).
- Riveted plating: alternate copper block and chiseled copper, or iron block with copper grate
  panels.
- Arched windows: 3-4 wide, stair-stepped brick arch, iron-bar mullions with panes.
- Catwalks: slab or iron trapdoor floor, iron bar railings, ladder access, hanging lanterns.
- Smokestacks: brick or nether brick, 3x3 or 4x4, banded with copper courses, slightly
  tapered, campfire inside for smoke.
- Clockwork: item frames with clocks, target blocks as valves, observers and pistons as
  machinery greebles, decorated pots as pressure vessels.
- Ground: cobbled deepslate paving, rails with minecarts, coal and iron block stockpiles,
  puddles, crates.

## Interiors

Open workshop floor with visible roof trusses (stripped logs and iron bars), a mezzanine
office with glass partitions, machinery banks, workbenches (crafting tables, anvils,
grindstones, smithing tables), pipe runs across the ceiling, warm copper-bulb lighting,
blackboard and map walls (item frames, lecterns, maps).

## Mistakes

- Copper used only as flat unoxidized sheets - use the full gradient and the 1.21 variants.
- Symmetric clean facades; this style wants asymmetry and accretion.
- Pipes that go nowhere - every run should start and end at something (tank, wall, machine).
- Sawtooth roof with the glazing on random sides; keep every glazed face pointing the same way.
- No vertical element - without a stack or tower it reads as a plain brick box.
- Glass panes placed against stairs or slabs, so they render as lone posts (see
  `references/block-connection-rules.md`).
