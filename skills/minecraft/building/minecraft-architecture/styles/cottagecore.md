# Cottagecore

A small, soft, over-planted cottage where the garden matters as much as the building. The
structure is deliberately modest; the charm comes from roof bulk, texture and layered
planting.

## Signature

Low eaves you can almost touch, a thatch-look roof taller than the walls, crooked timber, a
smoking chimney, and more plants than wall.

## Massing

- Tiny footprint, 7x9 to 11x13. One and a half storeys: ground floor plus a roof room.
- L-shaped plan with a lean-to for a porch or woodshed; never a plain rectangle.
- **Walls short, roof tall.** Wall 3-4 blocks, roof rise 5-7. The roof is the building.
- Attachments do the work: porch, bay window, dovecote, well, garden arch, drying line.
- Sink the foundation 1 block into the ground and let moss and path bury the plinth.

## Roofs

- Steep 1:1 or steeper, 2-block overhang on all four sides, no clean gutter line.
- Thatch look: hay bales as the mass, spruce or jungle stairs for ridge and eave courses,
  stripped log purlins showing under the overhang. Break the ridge with a bump or chimney so
  it never reads as a straight stair run.
- Alternative tile roof: bricks with brick stairs and a stripped-log ridge beam.
- Dormers are mandatory above 9 blocks wide - one gabled dormer per long side.
- Plant the roof: moss carpet (1.17+), azalea leaves and vines on the shaded slope.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Base ~60% | Whitewashed cob: white terracotta, bone block, calcite (1.17+), smooth sandstone; or mud bricks and packed mud (1.19+) for an earthier cottage |
| Secondary ~30% | Timber frame in oak, spruce or stripped birch logs; hay bales and spruce stairs for thatch |
| Accent ~10% | Bricks and brick stairs for the chimney, one saturated door or shutter colour |
| Trim | Stripped logs, fences, trapdoors as shutters, signs, lanterns |
| Glass | Glass panes in 2-wide openings with full-block jambs, always a flower box below |
| Light | Lanterns, candles (1.17+), a campfire hearth, glowstone under carpets |

Keep values warm and light, with exactly one saturated accent colour.

## Details

- Timber frame properly: post every 3 blocks, sill beam at floor level, head beam under the
  eave, one diagonal stair brace per bay.
- Flower boxes: trapdoor bracket under a row of flower pots, composters or cauldrons; pink
  petals (1.20+), wildflowers and leaf litter (1.21.5+) around the base.
- A wandering path of dirt path, coarse dirt and gravel - never a straight line.
- Props: composter, barrel, beehive with a real bee nest, hay-bale scarecrow with a carved
  pumpkin, laundry line of chains and white banners.
- Fence the plot at 1 block high with a gate that does not line up with the door.
- Vines, glow lichen (1.17+) and moss on one wall only, so the cottage weathers unevenly.

## Interiors

One room downstairs holding everything: campfire hearth under a chimney shaft, counter of
barrels and cauldrons, a fence-post-and-pressure-plate table, carpet rug. A ladder or 1-wide
stair to the roof room with the bed under the slope. Stripped-log ceiling beams every 3
blocks. Light at level 8-10 from hung lanterns and table candles so it reads as firelight.

## Mistakes

- A tall cottage. Above two storeys it is a farmhouse - cut a floor.
- A flat 45-degree stair roof with no thatch texture: the single biggest tell.
- Straight paths and square fences. Everything here wanders.
- Bright even lighting. Cottagecore is dim and warm.
- Bare ground. Budget as much effort for planting as for the building.
