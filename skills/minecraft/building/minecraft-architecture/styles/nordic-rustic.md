# Nordic / rustic

Log construction, steep roofs, turf and moss, built into the landscape. Heavy, low, warm,
weathered.

## Signature

- Long house form - length 2-3x the width, one dominant ridge.
- Very steep roof (2:1 or 3:1) that comes low, sometimes almost to the ground.
- Turf or shingle roof, carried on visible beams; heavy timber ends projecting at the gables.
- Stone base course, log walls above, minimal windows.

## Massing

- Primary long hall + a lower lean-to or shed on one long side (gives a saltbox roof).
- Gable ends face the approach; entrance on the long side under a small porch roof.
- Cut into a slope so one long wall is partly buried, or terrace the ground with retaining
  walls of cobble and stone.
- Boat houses, barns, drying racks and woodpiles as satellite masses.

## Roofs

Primary: very steep gable, half-hip, or saltbox. Secondary: shed, catslide (roof continuing
down over an addition).

- 2:1 pitch built from block + stair pairs; 3:1 for stave-church drama.
- Turf roof: moss blocks or grass blocks with a course of slabs below, azalea leaves and
  ferns on top, edged with a log purlin so the turf does not spill.
- Shingle roof: spruce stairs blended 70/30 with dark oak stairs, or deepslate tiles for
  stone-shingled roofs.
- Gable ends: projecting log purlins (3 courses), a carved head (stripped log + stairs), and
  crossed beams at the ridge.
- Overhang 2 blocks, with fence or log brackets.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Walls | spruce log, stripped spruce log, spruce planks, dark oak log accents, pale oak (1.21.4+) for weathered wood |
| Base | cobblestone, mossy cobblestone, stone, andesite, cobbled deepslate, gravel |
| Roof | moss block + grass block turf, spruce stairs, dark oak stairs, deepslate tiles, hay bale for barns |
| Accent | dark oak carved ends, iron blocks/bars, red or blue banners, resin bricks (1.21.4+) for warmth |
| Soft | moss carpet, ferns, tall grass, azalea, hanging roots, coarse dirt, podzol |
| Light | lanterns, campfires, soul lanterns for cold nights, candles |

Horizontal log courses: alternate `spruce_log` (axis sideways) with `stripped_spruce_log` in
bands of 2-3 to fake stacked timber; corner joints get a full log column.

## Details

- Corner posts of full logs standing proud of the wall plane.
- Few, small windows (1x2), deeply recessed, with shutters (trapdoors) and a heavy lintel.
- Porch: 2 deep, on two log posts, with its own steep roof and a bench of slabs.
- Woodpile under the eaves (stacked logs with axis facing out), drying rack of fences and
  chains with hanging items, barrels, a stone well, a smoker or firepit outside.
- Chimney of rough cobble, wide at the base, with smoke.
- Paths of gravel and coarse dirt, muddy near the door; stone stepping slabs across wet ground.
- Boats, nets, shields on the walls, antlers, banners for a Viking reading.

## Interiors

One long hall with a central hearth (campfire under a chimney opening), benches of slabs
along the walls, a raised sleeping platform, fur rugs (carpets), beams every 2 blocks,
low warm light, storage in barrels, smoke-darkened upper walls (swap to dark blocks near the
ceiling).

## Mistakes

- Shallow roof or thin roof edge - this style needs mass.
- Oak and birch as the main wood (too bright); spruce leads.
- Large windows and clean stone - keep openings small and surfaces weathered.
- Building on flat ground with no terrain cut or terracing.
- Turf roof with a hard straight edge and no lip.
- Forgetting the outbuildings; a lone long house looks unfinished.
