# Gothic

Cathedral logic: everything is a structural argument for **height**. Walls are reduced to a
skeleton of piers and buttresses, and the space between them becomes glass.

## Signature

- Vertical proportion 2.5:1 or taller (a 20-wide nave wants 50+ blocks of height).
- Pointed arches everywhere - openings, arcades, vaults. A round arch reads Romanesque instead.
- Buttresses stepping out from the wall every 5-7 blocks, with pinnacles on top.
- Glass is the wall: tall lancet windows in groups of 2-3, one rose window on the main facade.

## Massing

- Cross-shaped footprint (nave + transept + apse) for churches; L or T for civic buildings.
- Nave 9-15 wide, aisles 4-6 either side, 2-3 blocks lower.
- Towers: one asymmetric tower or two matched ones at the west front; spire height at least
  1.5x the nave height.
- Apse at the east end: half-octagon or half-circle (layer tables in
  `../../minecraft-organic-shapes/references/circles-and-cylinders.md`).
- Break the roofline with pinnacles, crockets and a crossing tower or fleche.

## Roofs

Primary: very steep gable, 2:1 to 3:1, over the nave; aisles get shed roofs leaning against
the clerestory wall. Towers take spires - octagonal cone, 2.5:1 minimum.

- Ridge line unbroken and dark; the drama comes from the pinnacles, not from dormers.
- Flying buttresses: an arch springing from a free-standing pier to the nave wall at
  clerestory height, 1 block thick, with a slab on top as the water channel.
- Spires: square base -> octagonal transition (2-3 layers of slabs/stairs) -> tapering cone,
  finial of a lightning rod, end rod or fence post with a lantern.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Base | deepslate bricks, cracked deepslate bricks, polished deepslate, stone bricks for lighter cathedrals |
| Secondary | blackstone, polished blackstone bricks, cobbled deepslate (weathered zones) |
| Accent | stained glass (purple, blue, red, orange), gold blocks for filigree, calcite for light stonework |
| Trim | deepslate tile walls, chiseled deepslate, polished deepslate slabs and stairs |
| Light | soul lanterns, candles, glowstone hidden behind stained glass, chains with lanterns |

A lighter gothic (French limestone look) swaps the base for calcite + diorite + smooth stone
and keeps the dark tier for the roof only. Do not mix both reads in one building.

## Details

- Pointed arch, 3 wide: stairs stepping inward, meeting at a single top block. 5 wide: two
  stair steps each side, then a 1-block apex. Arch rise should equal or exceed half the span.
- Arcade rhythm: pier (2 wide) - opening (3-4 wide) - pier, repeated, with the pier carried up
  the facade as a pilaster.
- Tracery in big windows: iron bars or deepslate walls dividing the glass into 2-4 lights,
  with a small circle of glass at the head.
- Rose window: a circle of diameter 9-13 (odd diameter for a real centre), stained glass with a
  wall/bar radial pattern, framed by a 1-block stone ring.
- Pinnacles: 1x1 stack of 3-5 blocks topped by a stair pyramid, one per buttress.
- Gargoyles: a 2-3 block projection of stairs and a carved head (stone brick + button eyes) at
  the parapet corners; pair with a chain for a water spout.
- Parapets with a battlement rhythm of wall blocks and gaps, or trefoil shapes from stairs.
- Statues in niches: 2-3 block recesses with a stair canopy above.

## Interiors

- Ribbed vaults over the nave: springing at 60-70% of the total interior height, with ribs of
  stairs meeting at a keystone (see
  `../../minecraft-organic-shapes/references/arches-and-bridges.md`).
- Clerestory windows above the aisle roofs are the main light source - keep the lower walls dark
  so the light reads as descending.
- Floor: polished andesite / polished deepslate chequer with a pattern down the central axis,
  tomb slabs and brass inlays (gold) along the aisles.
- Furniture: pews from stairs, altar on a raised podium, lectern, candles at 3-block spacing,
  organ from note blocks, bookshelves and chiseled bookshelves (1.20+) in the chapter house.
- Full interior method in `../../minecraft-interiors/SKILL.md`.

## Mistakes

- Building it short. Gothic at 3 floors of height is just a dark stone box.
- Round arches, or pointed arches with a rise under half the span (they look squashed).
- Buttresses as flat pilasters that do not project or step.
- Rose window on an even diameter, so there is no centre block.
- Stained glass used as whole walls of one colour instead of framed windows with patterns.
- Bright, even interior lighting - gothic interiors are dim with shafts of light.
- Battlements from stair blocks only; use full blocks with gaps so the silhouette is crisp.
