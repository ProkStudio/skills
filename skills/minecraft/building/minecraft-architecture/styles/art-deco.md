# Art deco

1920s-30s urban glamour: stepped towers, hard vertical emphasis, polished stone and metal,
and geometric ornament applied with discipline.

## Signature

Ziggurat setbacks, vertical piers running the full height, a symmetrical crowned entrance,
chevron and sunburst motifs, and backlit glass.

## Massing

- **Setbacks.** Build a wide base of 2-4 storeys, step back 1-2 blocks, continue, step back
  again. Three to five setbacks make a tower; two make a cinema or a bank.
- Strict bilateral symmetry on the main facade. This style does not tolerate asymmetry.
- A crowned centre: the central bay rises 3-6 blocks above the flanking mass and terminates
  in a stepped or finned cap with a spire or beacon.
- Vertical piers every 2-3 blocks, projecting 1 block, running unbroken from the base to the
  parapet - they read as pinstripes and are the core of the style.
- Corners can be rounded on large volumes; use the circle tables in
  `../../minecraft-organic-shapes/references/circles-and-cylinders.md`.

## Roofs

- Flat with a stepped parapet; the silhouette is made by setbacks, not slopes.
- Crown the piers: each pier ends in a 1-2 block stepped cap of slabs, so the parapet is a
  row of small towers rather than a flat line.
- A spire, mast or beacon column at the centre, in gold blocks or iron bars.
- Light the crown from below with sea lanterns or glowstone behind the parapet so the top
  glows at night.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Base ~55% | Smooth quartz, quartz block, white concrete for the cream masonry field |
| Piers and reveals ~25% | Quartz pillar and chiseled quartz for fluting; polished diorite for a cooler version |
| Shadow ~10% | Black concrete, polished deepslate and deepslate tiles (1.17+) for banding and the plinth |
| Metal accent ~10% | Gold blocks, raw gold blocks (1.17+), copper and chiseled copper (1.17+/1.21+) for bronze; iron bars for grilles |
| Glass | Tinted glass (1.17+), light blue and gray stained glass in tall strips; glass panes for shopfronts |
| Light | Sea lanterns and glowstone behind glass, end rods as vertical light lines, redstone lamps for signage |

Three materials maximum plus one metal. Discipline is the style.

## Details

- Fluting: alternate quartz pillar and chiseled quartz vertically inside each pier, or run
  quartz pillar blocks with the `axis` upright - see
  `../../minecraft-block-palettes/references/special-blocks.md` for orientation rules.
- Chevron and zigzag bands: stairs laid in alternating directions across a 1-block band above
  the entrance and under the parapet.
- Sunburst over the doorway: a fan of stairs and slabs radiating from the centre in gold and
  black, three to five rays each side.
- Entrance: recessed 2 blocks, 3-5 wide, framed in gold, with a marquee canopy of slabs and
  a signage band of redstone lamps or a hanging sign (1.20+).
- Windows as continuous vertical strips: glass panes flanked by the piers, with a dark spandrel
  panel between floors so the strip reads as unbroken height.
- Ornament goes in three places only: the crown, the entrance, and the spandrel band. Empty
  wall between them is correct.

## Interiors

Lobbies, not rooms: double-height space, a symmetrical stair or a raised dais on the axis,
black and white floor pattern in concrete or terracotta, gold banding at dado height, and
indirect cove lighting from glowstone behind quartz slabs. Furniture is low and geometric -
slab benches, banner wall panels. Light bright and even, 12-14, unlike most styles here.

## Mistakes

- No setbacks: a flat-topped box with gold trim is not deco.
- Horizontal emphasis. Keep piers vertical and unbroken; horizontal banding only at the crown
  and entrance.
- Too many ornament types. Pick chevron *or* sunburst as the dominant motif.
- Asymmetry, off-centre doors, or a random skyline.
- Warm wood anywhere on the exterior; this palette is stone, glass and metal.
