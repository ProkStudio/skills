# Dwarven / underground

Architecture carved **out of** rock rather than placed on it. Scale is deliberately superhuman,
geometry is heavy and symmetrical, and every surface shows tool work.

## Signature

- Volumes read as excavated: rooms are cut into a rock mass, and the rock is always visible at
  the edges of the composition.
- Massive square piers (3x3 to 6x6) with carved capitals and bases, on a strict grid.
- Everything is oversized: doors 5-9 wide, halls 20-40 tall, stairs 5-11 wide.
- Heavy geometric ornament - chevrons, stepped frets, concentric rings - never organic curves.

## Massing

- Gate facade: a vertical cliff face carved into a monumental frontispiece, with the door at
  the bottom centre and 2 colossal statues flanking it (8-20 blocks tall).
- Great hall: long axial volume, 15-30 wide, piers in two rows, a throne or forge at the end.
- Vertical shafts: the signature interior - a 10-25 wide shaft dropping 40-80 blocks with
  balconies, bridges and stairs spiralling around the walls.
- Bridges across chasms without visible support at midspan (corbel out from both sides).
- Living quarters as small cells opening off galleries; industry (forge, mine, smelter) as the
  lowest and hottest level.

## Roofs

Mostly none - the ceiling **is** the rock. Where a roof exists:

- Stepped corbel ceilings: each course steps in 1 block, 4-8 courses, meeting a flat centre
  panel with a light source.
- Barrel vaults over corridors (arch tables in
  `../../minecraft-organic-shapes/references/arches-and-bridges.md`).
- Outside structures (gatehouses, watchtowers) get low pyramidal stone roofs or heavy slab
  cornices, never timber gables.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Base | blackstone, polished blackstone bricks, deepslate bricks, deepslate tiles, basalt |
| Secondary | polished deepslate, cobbled deepslate, stone bricks for older sections, tuff (1.21+) |
| Accent | gold blocks, gilded blackstone, iron blocks, copper (oxidation gradient), emerald and diamond blocks as treasure |
| Trim | chiseled polished blackstone, polished blackstone brick walls, chains, iron bars |
| Light | lava channels, magma blocks, shroomlight, lanterns on chains, soul lanterns for haunted sections, copper bulbs (1.21+) in workshops |

Rock context (the walls you carve into) stays raw: deepslate, tuff, andesite, gravel, with ore
blocks left exposed as decoration.

## Details

- Pier capitals: 2 courses of stairs facing out, then a 1-block band, then the ceiling spring.
  Bases mirror them. Carve a chevron band into the shaft with a contrasting block.
- Chevron and fret friezes running the length of every hall at 2 heights: above the pier
  capitals and at floor level.
- Doors: a 5-9 wide opening framed by a 2-block-deep arch order (three concentric rings of
  stairs), with an iron-bar portcullis and a gold-inlay lintel panel.
- Statues: 8-20 blocks, squared-off armoured figures holding axes or hammers, placed in pairs
  and facing the axis (build method in
  `../../minecraft-organic-shapes/references/natural-forms.md`).
- Lava as architecture: channels 1-2 wide in the floor with iron-bar grates over them, cascades
  behind a forge, glowing cracks in the walls (magma under a layer of gravel).
- Mining evidence: scaffolding, minecart rails, chiseled tool marks (alternating polished and
  rough blocks), spoil heaps of gravel and cobble, abandoned wings without light.
- Water as contrast: a still black pool (dark prismarine floor) or a cistern fed by dripstone.

## Interiors

- Ceiling height is the whole style - never let a major room be under 8 blocks tall.
- Floors: polished blackstone / deepslate chequer with a gold or copper inlay strip down the
  axis; gravel and dirt in the mining levels.
- Forge: blast furnaces and smokers in a bank, anvils on stone plinths, lava under grates,
  chains and hoppers overhead, tool racks from item frames or shelves (1.21.9+).
- Feast hall: long tables (slabs on barrels), banners between piers, huge hearth, barrels of
  ale, cauldrons.
- Treasury: gold/emerald/diamond blocks in mounded stacks, copper chests (1.21.9+), lanterns low
  so the metal glows.

## Mistakes

- Human-scale proportions. A 3-block door and a 5-block ceiling kill the style instantly.
- Building a stone box above ground and calling it dwarven - the rock context must be visible.
- Perfectly clean rock walls: carve them unevenly, leave ore and gravel, add dripstone.
- Curved organic detail; keep ornament geometric and repeating.
- Uniform bright lighting. Contrast between glowing forge and dark gallery is the mood.
- Gold everywhere. Keep precious metals to inlays, statue detail and the treasury.
- Forgetting circulation: without stairs, bridges and galleries a big shaft reads as an empty
  hole.
