# Sci-fi / futuristic

Clean primary volumes, machined detail, emissive light lines. The failure mode is not
ugliness - it is emptiness. Futuristic builds need **greebling**: dense small-scale mechanical
detail against large calm surfaces.

## Signature

- Simple bold masses (slab, cylinder, ring, wedge) intersecting at clean angles.
- Panel lines: continuous 1-block grooves of a darker block dividing every large surface.
- Emissive strips instead of lamp fixtures - light reads as part of the structure.
- Greebles: pipes, vents, grates, antennae, hatches - concentrated at joints and undersides.

## Massing

- Pick one geometry family per build: orthogonal (blocks and slabs), radial (domes, rings,
  cylinders - see `../../minecraft-organic-shapes/SKILL.md`), or diagonal (45-degree wedges).
- Cantilever something. An overhang of 4-10 blocks over a void or water instantly reads as
  "engineered".
- Break the box by subtraction: carve notches, recess whole bays, cut a slot through the volume.
- Repeat a module (a pod, a bay, a fin) 3-7 times with one deliberate variation.
- Scale anchors matter more than in any other style: doors, railings, ladders and lit strips
  tell the player whether a white volume is a hut or a hangar.

## Roofs

- Flat roofs as **equipment decks**: vents, AC units (iron blocks + iron bars), dishes, solar
  panels (tinted glass or blue glass over a grid), railings, walkways of iron trapdoors.
- Domes and half-cylinders for habitats and reactors (layer tables in the organic-shapes skill).
- Wedge and shed planes at 1:3 for aerodynamic shapes.
- Parapet detail: a course of iron bars or copper grate (1.21+) set back 1 block, lit from below.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Base | white concrete, light gray concrete, smooth quartz, quartz block |
| Secondary | iron blocks, polished deepslate, gray concrete, black concrete for panel lines |
| Accent | cyan/light blue/lime stained glass, prismarine bricks, dark prismarine, copper grate (1.21+) |
| Trim | iron bars, iron trapdoors, chains, end rods, polished basalt |
| Light | sea lanterns, end rods, glowstone behind tinted glass, redstone lamps, copper bulbs (1.21+), soul lanterns for cold sci-fi |

Colour discipline: one emissive hue for the whole build (cyan for clean, orange for industrial,
magenta for retro-futurism). Two competing hues is the fastest way to make it look cheap.

Grittier variants: swap white concrete for gray + polished basalt + copper for a used-future
look, add rust (weathered copper, orange terracotta patches) and exposed structure.

## Details

- Panel lines: every 5-9 blocks, a single course of black concrete or polished deepslate,
  recessed 1 block where possible. Align them across masses so they read as one grid.
- Light strips: a 1-block channel with a sea lantern or glowstone at the bottom, covered by a
  trapdoor, iron bar or tinted glass so the source is hidden; run them along floor edges,
  under overhangs and up corners.
- Greeble recipes: iron bars over a black concrete recess (vent); chains with a lightning rod
  (antenna); hoppers and cauldrons under a floor (machinery); copper grate over glowstone
  (reactor panel); observers and dispensers as instrument faces; end rods as thruster nozzles.
- Glass: full blocks or tinted glass for curtain walls (no panes - panes read as fences at
  distance); frame every glazed bay with a 1-block iron or dark border.
- Airlocks and blast doors: 3-5 wide iron-block frames with an iron trapdoor or piston door
  (mechanism in `../../../redstone/minecraft-redstone-for-builders/references/hidden-doors.md`).
- Landing pads: circle of light gray concrete with a chevron pattern, end rods as markers,
  a lit ring, and chains anchoring the deck.
- Cables and conduits: chains, lightning rods and iron bars running along ceilings and down
  walls, entering the structure through a visible port.

## Interiors

- Corridors with a lit floor line, panelled walls (trapdoors or grates as panelling) and a
  visible ceiling service run.
- Rooms as functional sets: bridge (lecterns and observers as consoles, glass front), medbay
  (white concrete, beds, brewing stands), hangar (huge doors, painted floor markings), quarters
  (bunk beds of slabs and carpets), reactor (glass core, prismarine, warning stripes).
- Floor markings: yellow and black concrete stripes for hazard zones, painted walkway edges.
- Keep two thirds of interior surfaces plain white/grey so screens, lights and machinery read.
- Full interior method in `../../minecraft-interiors/SKILL.md`.

## Mistakes

- A clean white box with no greebles, no panel lines and no scale anchors.
- Glass panes as windows; use full glass or tinted glass.
- Mixing three emissive colours.
- Visible torches or lanterns - all light must look engineered.
- Symmetry everywhere with no functional asymmetry (all real machines have one side with the
  access hatch).
- Detailing every surface to the same density, which flattens the whole build.
- Ignoring the site: put it on a terraformed platform, a crater rim, water or a cliff, and cut
  the terrain around it with the terraforming skill.
