# Mesoamerican

Stepped stone platforms in dense jungle: talud-tablero banding, steep ceremonial stairways,
heavy carved masks, and vegetation reclaiming everything that is not maintained.

## Signature

- **Talud-tablero**: a sloped batter (talud) topped by a vertical recessed panel (tablero),
  repeated up the whole structure. This band is the style's fingerprint.
- Stepped pyramid platforms with a temple structure on the summit.
- Stairways far steeper than anything comfortable - 1:1, running the full height, with heavy
  balustrades (alfardas) either side.
- Plaza-centred planning: buildings define open squares, not streets.

## Massing

- Pyramid: square or rectangular base 15-40 wide, 3-9 receding tiers, each tier 2-4 blocks tall
  and stepped back 2-3 blocks.
- Summit temple: small (5x7 to 9x11), tall doorway, and a **roof comb** - a decorative wall
  rising 3-8 blocks above the roof, often pierced with holes.
- Palace/range buildings: long low volumes on a 2-4 block platform, many identical doorways.
- Ball court: two parallel sloped-wall ranges with an alley 4-7 wide between them, stone rings
  high on the walls.
- Group buildings around plazas of 20-40 blocks with a small altar platform at the centre.

## Roofs

- Flat roofs over corbel-vaulted interiors (see
  `../../minecraft-organic-shapes/references/arches-and-bridges.md` for the corbel steps).
- Roof combs are the vertical drama: 1-2 blocks thick, pierced with 1x2 openings, carrying
  masks or a stepped fret silhouette.
- Thatch (hay bales + dried kelp blocks) on domestic buildings with a 2:1 pitch and deep eaves.
- Cornices: two stair courses projecting 1 block each, running the full length of each tier.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Base | stone bricks, mossy stone bricks, cracked stone bricks, cobblestone, andesite |
| Secondary | chiseled stone bricks (carved panels), polished andesite, tuff (1.21+) for variation |
| Accent | green and lime terracotta, prismarine and dark prismarine (jade), gold blocks, red terracotta for painted plaster |
| Trim | stone brick walls, stone brick stairs, mossy cobblestone walls |
| Light | campfires, lanterns, candles, glowstone hidden under carpets; torches in survival |

Overgrowth kit: moss block, moss carpet, vines, glow lichen, hanging roots, azalea, big
dripleaf, jungle leaves. Apply to the north/shaded faces and the lower third only.

## Details

- Talud-tablero band, block by block: 2-3 courses stepping in 1 each (talud), then a 2-block
  vertical panel set back 1 with a stair frame top and bottom (tablero). Repeat per tier.
- Stair balustrades: a solid 2-block-wide ramp wall either side of the stairway, ending in a
  carved head (stone brick + chiseled block + button eyes) at the bottom.
- Serpent columns at temple doorways: a 2x2 pillar with a stepped head at the base.
- Masks: 3x3 or 5x5 panels built from chiseled stone bricks, stairs for the brow and fangs,
  green terracotta or prismarine eyes, one per tier corner or flanking the stairs.
- Stepped fret (greca) pattern: a repeating 3x3 L-shape in a contrasting block along friezes.
- Stelae: 1x3 to 2x5 standing slabs with carved faces, set in rows in front of the pyramid.
- Altars: low 3x3 platforms with a slab top, braziers, cauldrons.
- Sacbe (white road) linking structures: 3-5 wide raised causeway of calcite, diorite or smooth
  stone, 1-2 blocks above the plaza.

## Interiors

- Small, dark, corbel-vaulted rooms - interiors are deliberately cramped compared to the mass.
- One doorway as the only light source; add a hidden glowstone under a carpet for playability.
- Walls: plaster (white or red terracotta) with painted bands, a few chiseled panels.
- Furnishings: stone benches (slabs on walls), braziers, decorated pots (1.20+), chests as
  offering boxes, gold blocks and emerald blocks as treasure, sculk or amethyst for a ritual
  chamber.
- Full interior method in `../../minecraft-interiors/SKILL.md`.

## Mistakes

- Smooth-sided pyramids with no talud-tablero banding - it becomes an Egyptian pyramid.
- Gentle, comfortable stairways; the steepness is the point.
- Symmetrical, even overgrowth. Vegetation goes on shaded faces, lower courses and abandoned
  wings only.
- Only cobblestone. Blend three stone textures or the mass reads flat at distance.
- Placing pyramids in open plains; the style needs jungle, karst hills or dense planting around
  it - use the terraforming skill for the setting.
- Roof comb omitted, leaving a plain box on the summit.
- Modern-sized windows. Openings are doorways and slots, rarely windows.
