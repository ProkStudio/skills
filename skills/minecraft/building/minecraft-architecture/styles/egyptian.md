# Egyptian

Mass, taper and repetition. The style is about walls that lean inward, gateways that dwarf the
player, and surfaces covered in carved panels.

## Signature

- **Battered walls**: every major wall tapers inward as it rises, 1 block in per 4-6 up.
- Pylon gateways: two tapering towers flanking a smaller doorway, the tallest thing on site.
- Flat roofs with a heavy cavetto cornice (a projecting slab-and-stair lip) at the top.
- Repetition at scale: rows of identical columns, sphinxes, obelisks along an axis.

## Massing

- Strict axial plan: avenue -> pylon -> open court -> hypostyle hall -> dark sanctuary, each
  space smaller and darker than the last.
- Pylons 12-25 tall, 6-10 wide each, with a 4-6 wide gate between them.
- Hypostyle hall: a forest of columns on a 4-5 block grid, centre aisle columns 2 blocks taller
  with clerestory slots between the roof levels.
- Pyramids and mastabas: square base, 1:1 to 2:1 slope, stepped or smooth-faced with a cap.
- Obelisks: 1x1 or 2x2, 12-25 tall, tapering 1 block near the top, gold pyramidion.

## Roofs

Flat, always. The interest is at the parapet:

- Cavetto cornice: a course of stairs facing outward, then a slab overhang, then a full block
  band - 3 layers total, projecting 1-2 blocks.
- Roll moulding: a line of walls or a chiseled band down each outer corner of a pylon.
- Roof terraces with low parapets, ramps instead of stairs for the main ascent.
- For pyramids: either full stepped courses (2-block steps) or a smooth casing of smooth
  sandstone with the top 20% in a contrasting light block.

## Palette

| Tier | Blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Base | sandstone, cut sandstone, smooth sandstone, chiseled sandstone |
| Secondary | red sandstone family for warmth, packed mud and mud bricks for domestic buildings |
| Accent | gold blocks, lapis blocks, blue and cyan terracotta, dark prismarine |
| Trim | sandstone walls, smooth sandstone stairs and slabs, chiseled sandstone panels |
| Light | lanterns, campfire braziers on gold-block bases, torches in wall brackets |

Note: cut sandstone has a **slab only** (no stairs), smooth sandstone has stairs and slabs. Use
chiseled sandstone as the "carved relief" block - it is the only sandstone with a figure motif.

## Details

- Wall relief: alternate 1-block-deep recessed panels with chiseled sandstone, 3-4 wide, in a
  continuous band at eye level.
- Columns: papyrus (1x1 sandstone shaft with a wall block below the capital, capital of stairs
  facing out), lotus bud (slab + stair cap), palm (moss carpet or azalea leaves ring under a
  slab), or plain square piers 2x2 for the biggest halls.
- Gates: 4-6 wide opening with a lintel of 2 solid blocks, flanked by statues, with a winged-sun
  motif above (gold block centre, quartz or terracotta wings).
- Avenue of sphinxes: a repeated 3x5 block creature, one every 6 blocks, facing the axis.
- Cartouches: 2x3 gold-block panels with lapis or terracotta inlay on the pylon faces.
- Ramps in place of stairs, 3-5 wide, made of full blocks with a slab bevel at the edges.
- Courtyards with palm clusters (jungle logs + jungle leaves in an umbrella), reflecting pools
  lined with dark prismarine, and canal/well access to a river.

## Interiors

- Light gradient down the axis: bright court, dim hall, near-dark sanctuary lit by one hidden
  source on a gold statue.
- Ceilings painted dark (blue terracotta or dark prismarine) with gold accents to read as a
  night sky.
- Floors: smooth sandstone with a chiseled or terracotta border following the walls.
- Furnishings: sarcophagi from chiseled blocks and slabs, chests with gold-block plinths, papyrus
  scroll shelves (chiseled bookshelves, 1.20+), braziers from campfires in cauldron-style rings.
- Hidden chambers and dead-end passages fit the style and make a build memorable.

## Mistakes

- Vertical walls. Without the inward batter the build is just a sandstone cube.
- Using only one sandstone block type - the family has four textures plus the red variants, use
  at least three.
- Modern-proportioned doorways. Egyptian openings are 3-6 wide and 4-8 tall.
- Pyramids at 45 degrees on flat ground with no complex around them (temple, causeway, wall).
- Gold used as a surface rather than an accent; keep it under 10%.
- Random hieroglyph spam over every block instead of a controlled band.
- Forgetting the desert context: add dune build-up against the walls, drifted sand in corners,
  and dry vegetation only in the shaded side (see the terraforming skill for the dune rules).
